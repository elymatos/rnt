## 🧠 What Are the “What” and “Where” Pathways?

These are two major **cortical processing streams** that originate in the **primary visual cortex (V1)** and **diverge** to serve different cognitive functions:

### 🔵 The **“What” Pathway**

- Also called the **ventral stream**
    
- Runs from **V1 → V2 → V4 → Inferotemporal cortex (IT)**
    
- **Function**: Object recognition, shape, color, texture
    
- Answers the question: **“What am I seeing?”**
    
- Involves **fine visual detail**, pattern recognition, faces, words, and memory associations
    
- Strongly linked to **semantic memory**, **category learning**, and **identity**.
    

### 🔴 The **“Where” Pathway**

- Also called the **dorsal stream**
    
- Runs from **V1 → V2 → V3 → MT (middle temporal) → Posterior Parietal Cortex (PPC)**
    
- **Function**: Spatial location, movement, coordination
    
- Answers the question: **“Where is it?” and “How do I interact with it?”**
    
- Involves **depth**, **motion**, **spatial orientation**, and **action planning** (e.g., reaching, grasping)
    
- Strongly linked to **sensorimotor integration**, **navigation**, and **attention**.
    

---

## 🧭 Analogies

  
|Feature|Ventral “What” Stream|Dorsal “Where” Stream|
|---|---|---|
|Vision Goal|Identify object|Locate object / guide action|
|Speed|Slower, detailed|Faster, motion-sensitive|
|Memory Link|Semantic (e.g., names)|Sensorimotor & spatial maps|
|Associated Lobes|Temporal lobe|Parietal lobe|
|Lesion Effect|Visual agnosia|Optic ataxia or spatial neglect|

---

## 🧠 Columnar Mapping

Each of these streams is made of **cortical columns** processing:

- Local sensory features at lower levels (V1, V2)
    
- Composite features and invariance at higher levels (V4, IT, PPC)
    

They follow the same principles we’ve discussed:

- **Bottom-up input → prediction error**
    
- **Top-down feedback → contextual modulation**
    
- **Hierarchical temporal memory → compositional inference**
    

---

## 🔁 Cross-Talk and Integration

While the two pathways are functionally distinct, they **interact**:

- For example, to **reach for an object**, you must know **what** it is and **where** it is.
    
- **Prefrontal cortex** integrates both pathways to support decision-making and planning.
    

---

## 📍 Beyond Vision

The **what/where distinction** extends beyond vision:

- **Auditory system**: What = identifying sounds (speech, music); Where = spatial localization
    
- **Somatosensory**: What = texture or object recognition by touch; Where = body position & spatial awareness
